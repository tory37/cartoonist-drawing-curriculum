10Caveats worth remembering

  • The “skip” advice is contested, not settled. Some experienced Drawabox students argue the full construction sequence — including the box challenge — pays off in any style, comics included. Treating it as optional here is a deliberate trade-off for your time budget, not a fact everyone agrees on.
  • Free-book legality varies by title and country. Treat the Internet Archive as the safest free reading source for Loomis and Norling; buy in print if you want certainty. McCloud’s books are in copyright — use a library.
  • Some “free” resources have paid upsells. Proko and the Solo Art Curriculum surface a lot of free content but also sell premium courses. Drawabox itself is fully free, with an optional paid critique tier.
  • Links and channels change. If a specific video or playlist has moved, search the creator’s name directly — the recommendation still stands even when the URL doesn’t.
  • This is a synthesis of what free resources and their communities recommend, not a guarantee of outcomes. Progress depends almost entirely on consistent, enjoyable practice sustained over years.
