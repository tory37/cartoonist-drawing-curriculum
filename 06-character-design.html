06Character design & stylization

Etherington Brothers, Toniko Pantoja, Sycra · ongoing, months
  • Etherington Brothers, “How to THINK When You Draw” — 300+ free tutorials, including “How to draw CHARACTERS (3-Shapes)” and “(Flipped-Shapes).” The richest free cartoonist library on the web.
  • Toniko Pantoja (YouTube) — story/animation artist (How to Train Your Dragon 3, Trolls, Croods 2); excellent free videos on appealing shape language.
  • Sycra (YouTube) — “iterative drawing” and shape-design; great for developing your own voice through variation rather than copying.
  • Study your north stars directly. Copy frames from Adventure Time, Invader Zim, and similar cartoons. Break each character into its underlying simple shapes — Finn is a rounded box, Jake is a fluid blob.
exercises Shape-language studies (design the same character from circles, then squares, then triangles), expression sheets, and model sheets — the same character from multiple angles, which is exactly where your Phase 1–2 construction work pays off.
