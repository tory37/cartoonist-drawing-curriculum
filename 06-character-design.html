06Character design & stylization

Etherington Brothers, Toniko Pantoja, Sycra · ongoing, months
  • Etherington Brothers, “How to THINK When You Draw” — 300+ free tutorials, including “How to draw CHARACTERS (3-Shapes)” and “(Flipped-Shapes).” The richest free cartoonist library on the web, made by two working comic artists (Robin & Lorenzo Etherington, The Phoenix comic).
  • Toniko Pantoja (YouTube) — story/animation artist (How to Train Your Dragon 3, Trolls, Croods 2); excellent free videos on appealing shape language.
  • Sycra (YouTube) — “iterative drawing” and shape-design; great for developing your own voice through variation rather than copying.
  • Study your north stars directly. Copy frames from Adventure Time, Invader Zim, and similar cartoons. Break each character into its underlying simple shapes — Finn is a rounded box, Jake is a fluid blob.

A real, repeatable design drill — not a one-off

The Etherington Brothers’ 3-Shape Characters method is built to be run over and over, not done once: split a body into three sections, then randomly pick each section’s height (short/medium/tall) and width (narrow/medium/wide). Every combination produces a different, usable silhouette. Run it weekly with a fresh random combination — it’s how the Etheringtons themselves teach building a personal library of shapes fast.

exercises Run the 3-Shape drill weekly, then draw an expression sheet (and eventually a full turnaround model sheet) for whichever result you liked best that week — exactly where your Phase 1–2 construction work pays off.
