04Just enough perspective

ModernDayJames (YouTube), Norling’s Perspective Made Easy · roughly 3–6 weeks

Learn enough 1- and 2-point perspective to place a character in a room and draw a believable box, building, or prop. That’s the whole goal here.

skip / deprioritize Drawabox’s Lesson 6 (everyday objects), Lesson 7 (vehicles), the 25 Wheel Challenge, and 250 Cylinder Challenge. These are technical-perspective marathons for concept artists and industrial designers. A loose cartoonist fakes and stylizes perspective for expression — you don’t need measured vanishing-point precision.
