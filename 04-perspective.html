04Just enough perspective

Marshall Vandruff’s Perspective Drawing Series ($12) · roughly 3–6 weeks

Learn enough 1- and 2-point perspective to place a character in a room and draw a believable box, building, or prop. That’s the whole goal here.

The best perspective teacher most people never hear of

Marshall Vandruff’s 1994 Perspective Drawing Series — twelve one-hour lectures recorded live at Fullerton College — is widely considered some of the clearest perspective teaching ever put on video, and it’s $12 for the whole series. It explains the why behind the grid-building you just practiced in the construction module, taught as an actual lecture instead of a wall of procedural text. Preview his teaching style for free on his YouTube channel before buying.

skip / deprioritize Lectures 10–12 (Depth Measuring Systems and Plan Projection) are aimed at technical/product-design precision — measuring exact real-world distances into a drawing. Skip them; a cartoonist fakes and stylizes perspective for expression, not measured accuracy.
free alternatives ModernDayJames covers the same 1/2/3-point basics for free on YouTube in a looser, cartoon-friendly way, and Ernest Norling’s classic Perspective Made Easy (free and legal, Internet Archive) is a good plain-English companion if you’d rather read than watch or pay.
