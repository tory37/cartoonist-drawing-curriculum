Draw Your Own Comics

08Transition to digital

Procreate’s official Beginners Series · when you’re ready

When: only once you can construct and pose a character from imagination on paper — roughly the end of Phase 4, into Phase 5. Digital drawing is a separate motor and software skill; layering it on too early spends hours you don’t have to spare.

  • Procreate’s official “Beginners Series” — free, four-part, on Procreate’s own YouTube channel: brushes, layers, gestures, painting and editing tools.
  • Free beginner Procreate playlists from creators like Lisa Bardot.
  • Free alternatives if you’d rather not buy software yet: Krita (desktop) or Ibis Paint / Medibang (tablet).
first steps Re-do a few Phase 1 line/ellipse drills digitally to calibrate to the screen, learn layers (sketch → ink → color), and settle on two or three brushes you like. Don’t chase advanced rendering.

Color it: flatting basics

Once your layer workflow is comfortable, learn flatting — filling each area of your linework with a flat, solid color on its own layer before any shading. It’s the standard first step in digital comic coloring, and the difference between a coloring session that takes 20 minutes and one that takes 3: this Procreate walkthrough shows the selection-and-fill method.

keep it simple Pick 3–5 colors total for your first colored page. A limited palette forces decisions that actually read, and it’s far more forgiving than choosing from millions of colors with no plan.
