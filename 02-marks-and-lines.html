02Marks, lines & confidence

Scott Robertson & Thomas Bertling, How to Draw (Ch. 1 & 5) · roughly 3–6 weeks

Work Chapter 1 of How to Draw for confident, unbroken line work and proper markmaking, then jump ahead to Chapter 5, “Ellipses and Rotations,” for ellipse control. Robertson and Bertling both taught for years at Art Center College of Design, and the book explains the reasoning behind each drill instead of just assigning it.

why it matters This trains the single most transferable cartooning skill: a confident line and a clean shape drawn from the shoulder, not a scratchy, hesitant one.

Keep it light — a page or two per session as your structured half, then go draw cartoons for the rest.
