02Marks, lines & confidence

Drawabox Lesson 0–1 · roughly 4–8 weeks

Read Lesson 0 for the mindset and the 50% rule, then work Lesson 1: superimposed lines, ghosted lines and planes, tables of ellipses, and the basic perspective/box exercises.

why it matters This trains the single most transferable cartooning skill: a confident line and a clean shape drawn from the shoulder, not a scratchy, hesitant one.

Keep it light — a page or two per session as your structured half, then go draw cartoons for the rest.
