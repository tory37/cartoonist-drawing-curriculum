09Putting it together

Start this week

  • Grab a fineliner pen and cheap paper, and read the opening of Drawabox (module 01).
  • Start the Draw Like a Sir and Peter Han playlists (module 02), drawing whenever you can fit it in — even short sessions count — and mixing in fun cartoon doodling.
  • Consider Lynda Barry’s 4-minute diary — it needs no drawing skill and can run alongside everything else.

How you’ll know it’s time to move on

  • Past modules 02–03 — your lines are noticeably more confident and a box or cylinder “sits” believably in 3D. Don’t wait for perfection.
  • Don’t chase perfect construction before moving on. Once a simple object built from a couple of overlapping shapes reads believably, move to the perspective module and start applying it loosely — precision is a means, not the goal, for a cartoonist.
  • Spend the bulk of your years in modules 05–07 — gesture, character design, comics. This is what actually makes a cartoonist.

Adjust as you go

  • This plan gets edited as it’s worked through. If a resource isn’t working, look for a better one and swap it in — that’s how module 02 came to be built on two YouTube playlists instead of a textbook.
  • Dreading practice? Increase the play half, drop the most tedious exercise, draw more of your own characters.
  • Figures feel stiff or floaty? Double down on gesture (module 05) — the highest-leverage fix.
  • Already drawing confident shapes? Compress modules 02–04 and jump toward gesture and character design faster.
