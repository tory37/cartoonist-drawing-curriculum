03Basic construction & 3D forms

How to Draw (Ch. 2–4 & 6) · roughly 4–10 weeks

Work Chapters 2 through 4 of How to Draw — perspective terminology, technique, and building your first perspective grids — then Chapter 6, “Working with Volume,” for constructing solid 3D forms inside those grids. Understanding how simple volumes fit together in space is the backbone of “built from shapes” cartooning (a head as a rounded box, a body as a blob).

pace it This is the densest material in the book. Go slowly and work small — a page of simple boxes and cylinders sitting in a grid is plenty per session — and skip the book’s later vehicle/product-design chapters entirely. A loose cartoonist fakes and stylizes perspective for expression; you don’t need that level of measured precision.
