03Basic construction & 3D forms

Sycra (YouTube, free) · roughly 3–6 weeks

Watch Sycra’s “The Importance of Construction in Drawing” — free, and it teaches exactly the “built from shapes” thinking cartooning actually needs: run a simple line of action through a form, then wrap simple solids (spheres, cylinders, boxes) around it. No vanishing-point math required.

why it matters This is the mental model behind every cartoon character in this style: a head is a rounded box, a body is a blob or a sausage, a prop is two or three overlapping simple solids. Once this clicks, gesture and character design (the next two modules) get dramatically easier.

Practice on ordinary objects around you before jumping to characters — a mug, a shoe, a lamp. Simple, everyday subjects make the “shapes stacked in space” logic obvious.
