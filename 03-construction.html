03Basic construction & 3D forms

Drawabox Lesson 2 + optional partial Box Challenge · roughly 4–10 weeks

Work Lesson 2’s form and intersection exercises — understanding how simple volumes fit together in space is the backbone of “built from shapes” cartooning (a head as a rounded box, a body as a blob).

contested — read before skipping The full 250 Box Challenge is tedious for many students and a real burnout risk. Others argue the spatial payoff helps you rotate a character consistently across comic panels. Compromise: spread 50–100 boxes across several weeks as warm-ups instead of grinding all 250 in a block. Skip the deep texture-rendering drills — a cartoonist rarely needs realistic texture.
