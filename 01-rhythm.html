Draw Your Own Comics

01Weekly rhythm & supplies

Progress here comes from consistency, not intensity. Structure it like this:

  • Two sessions of 45–90 minutes a week (or one longer weekend block plus small doodling on the side).
  • Split every session roughly 50/50 — structured exercise from your current phase, then free “play”: doodle your own characters, copy cartoons you love, sketch from shows.
  • Don’t chase finished pieces early. The reps are the point, not the polish.
  • Expect each phase to take months. That’s normal, and it’s what makes this sustainable for five years instead of five weeks.

Supplies — keep it cheap

✎ HB pencil + a softer 2B/3B
✎ Vinyl or kneaded eraser
✎ Canson XL or Strathmore sketchbook
✎ Staedtler Mars Lumograph pencils
✎ A pack of fineliners (for Drawabox — ink only, no erasing)
✎ Printer paper is fine to start
